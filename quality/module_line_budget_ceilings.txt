# A budget in quality/module_line_budgets.txt may never exceed the ceiling
# recorded here. Shrinking a budget is always allowed; raising one past its

# reviewed migration in quality/ratchet_migrations.json.

# unenforced in practice. Six pins were raised that way (c8bff364d) and the
# green gate was reported as remediation. This ledger closes that hole: the
# approved debt is frozen, and new debt cannot be authorised by editing a
# number.
#

# The ceiling is the largest line count this module has ever been *approved* to
# have. It is a debt ceiling, not a target.
